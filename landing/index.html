A closer look

Made to disappear
into your flow.

A translator should feel like a reflex, not a destination. Dodoi opens over your work and gets out of the way.

Summon · type · translate

Three moves

In and out.
Nothing in between.

01

Summon

Press Shift + ~ anywhere. Dodoi appears on the display you're using.

02

Say it

Type or paste anything. Dodoi detects the source and keeps the tone, dialect, and formatting.

03

Move on

Hit Ctrl + ↵. The translation is copied and pasted back where you were typing.
